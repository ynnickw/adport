Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

Adport is an open-source, self-hosted tool for managing advertising accounts through a command-line interface and MCP server. This policy describes the limited information handled by the adport.dev website and the Adport software.

Website data

Adport.dev does not use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or user accounts. Our hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and request time, to deliver and secure the website.

Self-hosted software

Adport runs on infrastructure controlled by the user. Credentials, advertising account data, reports, pending operations, findings, and audit records are processed locally or by the advertising platforms the user connects. Adport does not send this information to Yannick Westermann Labs.

Advertising platform data

When a user connects Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, Apple Ads, or Microsoft Advertising, Adport accesses only the data and permissions needed to perform actions requested by that user. The connected platform processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. Adport does not sell advertising data or use it for profiling, unrelated advertising, or automated decisions about individuals.

Credentials and security

Users supply and control their own platform credentials. The open-source application stores credentials locally and uses them only to communicate with the selected advertising platform. Users are responsible for securing their systems, limiting access, and revoking credentials when access is no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

Because the software is self-hosted, users control retention and deletion of local data. Platform data may also remain with the connected advertising provider according to that provider’s policies. Standard website request logs, if retained by the hosting provider, are kept according to its security and retention practices.

International processing

Website infrastructure and connected advertising platforms may process information in countries other than the user’s country. The applicable provider’s terms describe its safeguards for international transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy as Adport develops. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to yannick@adport.dev.
